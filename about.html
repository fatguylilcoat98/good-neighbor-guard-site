Built from real life, not buzzwords.

My name is Chris Hughes. I'm a self-taught independent developer building practical technology under The Good Neighbor Guard.

My projects have started from very different problems: preserving family memories, understanding vehicle repairs, exploring reliable AI reasoning, creating private AI systems, and controlling what autonomous systems are allowed to do.

The common thread is simple: technology should give people more understanding, control, and confidence — not less.

That belief shows up differently in each project. In LYLO, it means helping families keep their stories. In AUBS, it means an AI that runs entirely on your own device and never sends your words anywhere. In VeraCore, it means testing whether an answer is actually supportable instead of just convincing. And in CLASPION, it means giving autonomous systems a hard boundary — a way to check whether an action should really be allowed before it happens.

I work independently. When I say "I built," I mean it — this is one developer's work, done carefully, with AI collaborators as tools, not a fictional engineering team. I'd rather tell you exactly what something is and isn't than dress it up.

"If it does not genuinely help people, it is not worth building."

Chris Hughes • The Good Neighbor Guard
