Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2026 · Short and honest.

Draft cookie information, pending attorney review. See also the Privacy page.

First-party cookies

This site sets no first-party tracking or advertising cookies. It runs no first-party analytics.

Local storage & caching

To support installability and offline use, the site stores static files in your browser's cache (via a service worker) on your device. This is not a tracking cookie and stays on your device. You can clear it any time from your browser.

Third-party cookies

Some pages embed YouTube videos. If you interact with an embedded player, YouTube/Google may set cookies and collect viewing data under their policies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives basic request data. These are third-party services with their own cookie practices.

Your control

Your browser lets you block or delete cookies and block third-party content. Doing so will not break the core site.

Changes

If cookie usage changes (for example, if privacy-conscious analytics is added), this page will be updated first.
