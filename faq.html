Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about The Good Neighbor Guard, CLASPION, VeraCore, and working together.

What is The Good Neighbor Guard?

The independent technology portfolio of Chris Hughes — a self-taught developer building practical, honest AI and software around people, trust, and accountability. Projects include CLASPION, VeraCore, AUBS, LYLO, and more.

Who builds these projects?

Chris Hughes, working independently. AI tools are used as collaborators, but this is one developer's work — not a large staff.

What is CLASPION?

A provider-agnostic execution-governance engine for agentic AI. It sits between an agent's intent and consequential execution, evaluates the action across truth, authority, actor-state, world-state, and risk, and returns a deterministic ALLOW/BLOCK decision, fail-closed, with a tamper-evident audit trail.

Is CLASPION open source?

No. CLASPION is proprietary. Its source and diligence materials are shared only under a written agreement. Nothing on this website grants a license to its source code.

Can I test CLASPION?

Yes — the CLASPION page has a safe, interactive sandbox that runs entirely in your browser. It illustrates how governance decisions look and performs no real action.

Is the public demo connected to production systems?

No. The public CLASPION sandbox and VeraCore examples run only in your browser. They are not connected to any production system, database, keys, or the applications those systems govern.

Can companies license or acquire CLASPION?

Yes. CLASPION is being explored for strategic acquisition, licensing, or integration. Qualified teams can request a technical overview and diligence package via the Partners page.

What is VeraCore?

An evidence-focused verification engine that tests whether an answer is supportable — using deterministic computation, multi-source consensus with enforced source diversity, and adversarial challenge — rather than just generating convincing text. It is in active development.

Can I partner with The Good Neighbor Guard?

Yes — integrations, licensing, research collaboration, proof-of-concept work, and acquisition are all open. See the Partners page.

Can I request source code?

Not through the website. Confidential source and diligence materials are shared only under a written agreement (typically after an NDA).

Are these systems production-ready?

It varies by project and is stated honestly on each. CLASPION is working late-beta — real and running, but not represented as a finished, certified enterprise product. Some projects are live apps; others are experimental.

How do I report a security issue?

See the Security page for responsible-disclosure guidance and contact. Please report privately and avoid accessing data that isn't yours.

Have a question that isn't here? Reach out via the contact page.
