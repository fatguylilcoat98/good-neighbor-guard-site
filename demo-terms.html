What the demos are

The CLASPION sandbox and VeraCore examples are sandbox / research demonstrations. They illustrate how the technology behaves. They run in your browser, are not connected to production systems, and must not be relied upon as a production decision-making or safety system.

You may not

  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to the site, demos, or any connected infrastructure.
  • Attempt to bypass, disable, or circumvent any control or limit.
  • Conduct denial-of-service activity or abuse any API or endpoint.
  • Submit injection or payloads intended to compromise infrastructure.
  • Upload or submit secrets, credentials, or personal/sensitive information (PII).
  • Attempt to trigger real-world destructive or consequential actions.
  • Use a demo as a production safety, compliance, or decision system.

No warranty

Demos are provided "as is," may be changed or removed at any time, and their outputs are illustrative — not guarantees. See the Terms of Use.
