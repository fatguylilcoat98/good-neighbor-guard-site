About

The Good Neighbor Guard is the independent technology portfolio of Chris Hughes, a self-taught developer based in Sacramento, CA. The work spans agentic-AI governance (CLASPION), evidence-focused AI verification (VeraCore), private on-device AI (AUBS), family memory (LYLO), and interactive AI applications.
