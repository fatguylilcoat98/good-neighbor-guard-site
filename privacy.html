Privacy

Last updated: 2026 · Plain-English summary of how this website handles information.

This is general website privacy information, not legal advice, and it is a draft pending attorney review. It describes the public marketing site at thegoodneighborguard.com. Individual apps (LYLO, AUBS, ROZTORD, etc.) have their own terms.

The short version

This site is a static marketing website. It does not run its own analytics, ask you to create an account, or store your submissions on its own servers. It does rely on a few standard third-party services (hosting, fonts, and embedded videos) that receive basic technical information when you load a page.

What is collected, and by whom

  • Hosting & delivery (GitHub Pages + Cloudflare). Like nearly all websites, the hosting and CDN providers automatically log basic request data (IP address, browser type, pages requested, timestamps) to serve pages and protect against abuse. This is standard server logging by those providers.
  • Fonts (Google Fonts). Typography is loaded from Google's font service, which receives your IP address and browser information when fonts load.
  • Embedded videos (YouTube). Some pages embed YouTube videos. YouTube/Google may set cookies and collect viewing data if you interact with an embedded player. We use the standard embed; playback only starts if you press play.
  • Contact / inquiry forms. The forms on this site open a pre-filled message in your own email application. Nothing you type is sent to or stored on this website. Your message goes only to the address in your email client when you choose to send it.
  • Interactive demos. The CLASPION sandbox and VeraCore examples run entirely in your browser. They send no data anywhere and are not connected to any production system.
  • Offline / install (service worker). To support installability and offline use, the site stores static files in your browser's local cache on your device. This stays on your device.

What is NOT collected

This site does not run first-party analytics, advertising trackers, or behavioral profiling. It does not sell personal information. It does not require login. If that changes, this page will be updated and — where a privacy-conscious analytics option is added — it will be disclosed here first.

Cookies

This site sets no first-party tracking cookies. Third-party embeds (notably YouTube) may set cookies if you interact with them. Your browser settings let you block or clear cookies.

Your choices

  • You can browse without submitting any personal information.
  • You can block cookies and third-party content in your browser.
  • You control whether to actually send any pre-filled contact email.

Data retention & security

Because this site doesn't collect submissions itself, there is no site-side database of your data to retain. Third-party providers retain their own logs per their policies. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure; this site is provided on a best-effort basis by an independent developer.

Children

This site is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

This policy may change as the site evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date.

Contact

Privacy questions: use the contact page. A dedicated privacy address will be published here once it is set up.
