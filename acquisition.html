Featured Opportunity

CLASPION

CLASPION is currently being explored for strategic acquisition, licensing, or platform integration. Qualified technical and business teams may request a technical overview, a demo, and a diligence package. It is a working, late-beta execution-governance engine for agentic AI — provider-agnostic, fail-closed, cryptographically audited, with ~500 automated tests, ~85% coverage, and live integration into two applications.
