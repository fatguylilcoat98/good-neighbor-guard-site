CLASPION

Execution governance for agentic AI — a control plane between an agent's intent and consequential execution. It evaluates proposed actions before they happen and returns deterministic ALLOW / BLOCK decisions, fail-closed, with a tamper-evident audit trail.

Try the Sandbox Discuss Acquisition / Integration

The gap most systems miss

Most AI safety lives in prompts and after-the-fact logging. That is not a control plane: it doesn't sit at the point of execution, it doesn't fail closed, and it can't prove why an action was allowed or blocked. As agents begin to take real actions — sending messages, spending money, calling APIs, changing systems — the hard question becomes "should this action be allowed to happen, right now, given everything we know?" CLASPION answers exactly that, deterministically and auditably.

How it works

The Master Invariant

"No action executes unless truth, authority, actor state, world state, and risk posture are all valid at the moment of execution — and remain valid through completion."

The core promise CLASPION enforces on every governed action.
1 · Basis-first

Every action is checked against the ground truth needed to decide. If the basis has collapsed, the answer is "no decision" — never a guess. No basis, no verdict.

2 · Evaluate the axes

Truth, Human-state, World-state, and Authority are each evaluated by a swappable "conscience" module, then resolved by the CLEAR decision engine.

3 · Grant & audit

If permitted, a cryptographically signed execution grant is issued, and every step lands in a hash-chained, tamper-evident log. A runtime monitor watches for drift until completion.

Security properties

Provable, not just plausible

  • Fail-closed by design — no basis, no verdict, no unverifiable "allow."
  • Cryptographic audit — hash-chained logs, replay-nonce protection, Ed25519-signed execution grants.
  • Append-only datastore — tamper-evidence enforced at the database.
  • Recursive Invariant — policy and authority changes are themselves governed actions.
Validation

Adversarially tested

  • ~500 automated tests, including a dedicated adversarial "Forge" harness with 100+ attack families.
  • ~85% measured code coverage (line + branch).
  • A tracked, published known-limitations manifest — the project tells you what isn't done yet.
  • Provider-agnostic: works under any agent or model; swap the policy "conscience" without changing the host.
Integration model

A bolt-on, not a rebuild

CLASPION is a bolt-on control plane. The host agent's reasoning, memory, and personality never enter it — only the action intent is submitted for validation. A consumer governs an action with a single call (in-process or over HTTP) and receives a decision plus a verifiable audit record. It is currently integrated into two independent applications as a live reference.

Honest maturity

Working late-beta — not a finished enterprise SaaS

CLASPION is real, tested, and running as the governance layer in two applications. It is late-beta: fully implemented and honestly documented, with a published list of known limitations and areas still being hardened. It is deliberately not represented as a completed, certified enterprise product. A qualified technical team can request a fuller technical overview and diligence materials.
